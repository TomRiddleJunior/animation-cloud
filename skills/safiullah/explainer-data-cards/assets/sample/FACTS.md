Phrasing notes
- VO says "the first UN treaty ratified by every country" — the UN's own framing; strictly it was the Protocol *and* its parent
  Vienna Convention together (same day). On-screen copy keeps it to "A FIRST IN UN HISTORY".
- "Every UN member state" is true; the 198 also include four non-member states and the EU, so the dot caption says
  "197 states and the European Union".
- Recovery years are projections conditional on current policies; the footnote says so.
- No quotes from any person; the document card paraphrases facts, it does not quote treaty text.
