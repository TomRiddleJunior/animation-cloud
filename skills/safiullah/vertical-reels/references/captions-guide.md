# Post captions: Instagram Reels and YouTube Shorts

Platform limits change, so confirm them at posting time. As of late 2026:
- **Instagram:** caption up to 2,200 characters, with about the first 125 shown before "more". Use 3–5 relevant hashtags (more reads as spam). Links in captions are not clickable.
- **YouTube Shorts:** title up to 100 characters; description up to 5,000. Hashtags in the description may show above the title. `#Shorts` is optional.

## Voice

Write calm and descriptive: say what the video is and what it shows. The owner finds pushy marketing copy ("you won't believe…", "INSANE") off-putting. Third person or plain statements work best. One line of curiosity in the hook is enough.

## Structure: Instagram Reels

1. **Hook line** (≤ 125 characters): what the video is, plus one concrete detail.
2. **2–4 short lines on what it shows.** For fact films, quote 2–3 headline facts **exactly as in the fact sheet**, with their source and date in brackets.
3. **How it was made.** One line: "Every number is sourced on screen" / "Built in code".
4. **Credits and disclosure:**
   - "Music: AI-generated (MiniMax-Music3)" or "Narration: AI voice (Kokoro TTS)";
   - "Researched and written with Claude (Anthropic)" when that's true;
   - footage or image credits by source family;
   - the original creator for a remake or homage.
5. **3–5 hashtags.**

## Structure: YouTube Shorts

- **Title:** about 60–90 characters, descriptive, with the subject first. Optionally end with `#Shorts`.
- **Description:**
  - the same hook and facts, with **sources and links**: the description can carry real URLs. Pick the on-screen sources of the facts you quote, or point to a full source list;
  - credits and disclosure;
  - 2–3 hashtags.

## Fact-film rules

- Quote only facts that **appear in the posted cut**. A Shorts cut may have dropped a section.
- Keep every qualifier the fact sheet requires: "projected", "unless extended", "in testing", the poll wording.
- Name no "winner". Keep the film's neutrality, and hold the research model's own maker to the same standard.

## Posting checklist (put it in each film's caption file)

- **Which file:** `<name>.shorts.reel.mp4` for Shorts and Reels (≤ 3:00); `.full.reel.mp4` for TikTok or a regular upload.
- **AI labels:**
  - YouTube Studio → "Altered or synthetic content" → **Yes**, when the video has AI vocals, an AI voice or AI images that could be taken as real.
  - Instagram → Advanced settings → **"Add AI label"**.
- **Licences:**
  - Mixkit *Restricted* clips are licensed for personal use only; re-pick them before posting a film publicly.
  - Qwen-Image plates are non-commercial only.
  - MiniMax's licence requires labelling the music as AI-generated.
- **Cover frame:** suggest 1–2 timestamps with a strong graphic plus the title.
- **Music on Instagram:** the audio is the film's own track. Do not add platform music over it.
