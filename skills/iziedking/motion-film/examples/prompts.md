# Sample prompts

Copy one, fill the brackets, and send it to your agent with this skill installed. Each asks for the brief and directions first, so you approve before anything renders or costs money.

## Recreate the craft of a reference

> Use the motion-film skill. Study this reference: [path or link]. Run the reference study, show me the shot list, cut rhythm, palette, type and sound notes, then a build plan that says which engine makes each part and what my machine can and cannot render. We rebuild the craft with our own assets: brand files at [path]. Do not reuse anything from the reference.

## Product ad with 3D inserts, 20 seconds

> Use the motion-film skill. Make a 20-second ad for [product]: a white stage, headlines arriving word by word, our real interface at large scale, and two or three 3D inserts ([e.g. spinning coins with our mark, tilted feature icons]). Channels: [X, Instagram, YouTube]. One benefit: [benefit]. Call to action: [action]. Brand: logo [path], font [path], colours [hex]. Truth table: [what is live, what is planned]. Generated shots: [allowed / not allowed]. Budget for paid tools: [amount or zero]. Check my machine first and tell me how long the 3D will take before you start.

## Advert, 15 seconds

> Use the motion-film skill. Make a 15-second advert for [product], for [X and Instagram], sound off by default. One benefit: [the benefit]. Call to action: [visit url / join the waitlist]. Brand: logo at [path], font [name] at [path], colours [hex list]. Truth boundaries: [what is live, what is planned]. Masters in 16:9, 9:16 and 1:1. Start with the brief and three directions from different style families.

## Launch film, 45 seconds

> Use the motion-film skill to plan a 45-second launch film for [product]. Story: one person, [their situation], the problem [specific friction], how [product] changes it. No voiceover; on-screen text carries it. Study these references for pacing only: [paths or links]. Show me three directions with time tables, engine plans and costs, then stop for my choice.

## 3D product shot

> Use the motion-film skill. Make a 6-second studio shot of [product]: dark ground, slow orbit, a light sweep across the surface, shallow focus. Model at [path to glb] (or build a simple stand-in and tell me). Render one test frame first and report the time. Transparent version too, so it can sit over our 2D film.

## Product walkthrough from the live app

> Use the motion-film skill to make a 60-second walkthrough of [feature]. Capture the real product at [url, already signed in / demo environment] for these steps: [steps]. Use demo data only. Add the camera moves, cursor and typing in the edit. Use authored scenes for how the money or data moves. Label anything not live as "Planned".

## Event sizzle from footage

> Use the motion-film skill. Cut a 30-second sizzle from the clips in [folder] to this track: [path] (licence: [licence]). Log the clips, show me your selects and the cut list, then render. Everyone identifiable has signed a release: [yes / these people have not]. Deliver 16:9 and a re-framed 9:16 with burned-in captions for any speech.

## Ranked list or gameplay compilation

> Use the motion-film skill. Build a "Top [N] [topic]" video from the clips in [folder]: a cold open, a title card, numbered segments with lower thirds, a recap. I have permission to use this footage: [details]. Authored number cards in our brand style. Normalise the sound per clip.

## Educational explainer, 2 minutes

> Use the motion-film skill. Turn this article into a 2-minute explainer: [paste or path]. Audience: [who]. Numbered chapters, one running example with consistent illustrative figures, a recap at the end. I will record the voiceover myself: write the script for speech, then key the timeline to my recording's word timings. Captions burned in and as .srt.

## Hand-drawn or illustrated piece

> Use the motion-film skill. Make a 30-second piece in a hand-drawn style: paper ground, wobbling line, a simple character who [does what], animated on twos at 24 fps. Palette: [colours]. Show me a 6-second study of the character and one scene change before the rest.

## Generated b-roll, with the rules

> Use the motion-film skill. I want three atmospheric shots ([describe]) generated for the opening. Before generating: tell me which model you would use today and why, its commercial terms, the estimated cost with retakes, and what label the film will need where it runs ([territories]). Keyframe stills first for my approval. Our logo and product are composited from real files, never generated.

## Motion study only

> Use the motion-film skill. Before the full film, build a 7-second motion study of the hardest transition: [describe it]. Final finish, with sound. I want to approve the look and easing first.

## Social cuts from an existing master

> Use the motion-film skill. Re-compose the film in [path to source] for 9:16 and 1:1. Re-lay out each scene for the frame; keep text and faces out of the platform's interface zones and show me the safe-zone check; do not centre-crop.

## Finish and deliver

> Use the motion-film skill to finish [path]: one grade and one grain pass across all shots, mix to -14 LUFS, a ProRes master, then deliveries for [YouTube 16:9, Reels 9:16, a 2 MB web hero loop]. Run QC on every file and give me the provenance ledger.

## Review and QC an existing video

> Use the motion-film skill's QC checklist on [path to mp4]. Run the measured checks, make a contact sheet and transition strips, and list every problem with its timestamp.

## What can this machine do

> Use the motion-film skill. Run the toolchain check and tell me which kinds of film I can make on this machine today, what I would need to install for the rest, and what would need a GPU or a paid service.
